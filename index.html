SOH CAH TOA Initial Velocity Decomposition

The launch vector \(\vec{v}_0\) acts as the hypotenuse of a right triangle at angle \(\theta\). Its horizontal motion is completely independent of its vertical free-fall.

CAH • Horizontal Component
\[\cos\theta = \frac{v_{0x}}{v_0} \implies v_{0x} = v_0 \cos\theta\]
v₀ₓ = 18.0 × cos(45°) = 12.73 m/s
SOH • Vertical Component
\[\sin\theta = \frac{v_{0y}}{v_0} \implies v_{0y} = v_0 \sin\theta\]
v₀ᵧ = 18.0 × sin(45°) = 12.73 m/s
TOA Check: \(\tan\theta = \frac{v_{0y}}{v_{0x}} \implies \theta = \arctan\left(\frac{v_{0y}}{v_{0x}}\right) = 45.0^\circ\). Notice that \(v_{0x}\) never changes during the entire flight because horizontal acceleration \(a_x = 0\)!
